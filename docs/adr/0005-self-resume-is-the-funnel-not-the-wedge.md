# Self-resume is the funnel, not the wedge

We let a developer's own agent query their *own* past **Sessions** ("self-resume" — distinct from **Handoff**, which reconstructs a *teammate's* in-progress work). But self-resume is strictly the free, unbranded solo on-ramp — never a paid feature, never the positioning. The defensible business stays **Handoff** + **Ask-why**; self-resume exists only to drive daily engagement, keep **Capture** running, and sweeten the personal-value side of being captured (you're captured for the team anyway; here's a personal win from the same data).

## Considered and rejected

- **Lead on / charge for self-resume.** Tempting, because it is the *daily* use while Handoff is weekly-per-team at best — so it would dominate engagement. Rejected: self-resume is the "personal agent memory" commodity row in our own competitive table, and the platforms (Claude Code / Cursor native cross-session memory) will give it away free and better-integrated. Building our identity or our price on it cedes the product to the platform — and the thing users touch daily becomes the thing they think we *are*, so daily use must point at something defensible.

## Consequence

Engagement metrics will be dominated by self-resume (daily) over Handoff (weekly). **Instrument the two separately from day one** and judge the wedge thesis on Handoff alone — otherwise commodity engagement reads as validation of the defensible product, a false positive that could cost a year. See [ADR 0002](./0002-top-down-org-purchase-handoff-led-wedge.md) for why re-leading on a different View is expensive to reverse.
